support

need help? email exquisitesketch@gmail.com. we usually reply within a few days.

how to play

  1. player 1 draws a head. don’t show the others. draw to the bottom, then pass the device.
  2. player 2 sees a piece of player 1’s drawing and draws the body. pass the device again.
  3. player 3 does the same with the legs.
  4. behold your exquisite creature. it saves to the gallery.

where are my drawings?

in the gallery, inside the app. they’re saved only on your device, so they don’t move to a new phone. to keep one, open it in the gallery and save it to your photos.

i deleted the app. can i get my drawings back?

sorry, no. they lived only on your device, and we never had a copy.

can i play with fewer or more than 3 people?

the game is built for 3 turns. with 2 people, one of you can take two turns.

something’s broken

email exquisitesketch@gmail.com and tell us your device, your iOS version, and what happened. a screenshot helps.
