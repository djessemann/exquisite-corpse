exquisite sketch

privacy policy

last updated november 23, 2026

the short version: exquisite sketch doesn’t collect any data about you. not your name, not your email, not your drawings, nothing.

the app

  • there are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • your drawings are saved only on your device, in the app’s gallery. we never see them. if you delete the app, they’re deleted too.
  • if you choose to save a drawing to your photos or share it, the app asks for permission first. it only adds the drawing you picked. it doesn’t read your photo library.
  • the app doesn’t need an internet connection to play.

this website

  • no cookies, no analytics, no forms.
  • like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. we don’t use them to track you.

apple

you download the app through the app store, which is run by apple under apple’s privacy policy.

kids

we don’t collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

changes

if this ever changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top before it does.
